VAML Two-Computer Acoustic Evidence Capture

This mode records the raw physical microphone signal on Computer B while Computer A sends the fixed three-burst VAML probe. It deliberately keeps the raw PCM evidence so the channel can be analyzed independently of the browser decoder.

Order: On Computer B click Arm Evidence Receiver. Wait until it says LISTENING — SEND NOW. Then on Computer A click Send Evidence Probe. Do not send during calibration. Keep the machines 10–30 cm apart for this evidence run and keep the sender volume unchanged from the previous test.
