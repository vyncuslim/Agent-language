VAML Two-Computer Acoustic Evidence Capture + Channel Calibration

This mode first measures the real speaker → air → microphone channel with a fixed calibration sequence, then records the raw physical microphone signal on Computer B while Computer A sends the fixed three-burst VAML probe. The saved WAV always contains the raw microphone evidence, never a processed signal.

Order: 1) On Computer B click Arm Calibration Receiver. 2) When it says CALIBRATION LISTENING, click Send Calibration on Computer A. 3) Wait for CALIBRATION PASS / DEGRADED on B, then save the calibration JSON and load it on Computer A. 4) On B click Arm Evidence Receiver; when it says LISTENING — SEND NOW, click Send Calibrated Evidence Probe on A. Keep the same speaker, volume, distance and AudioContext for calibration and probe. Do not change the volume in between.
